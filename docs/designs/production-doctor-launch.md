# Design: Production Readiness & Launch for Clinics and Doctors

## Problem Statement
Small-to-medium clinics and solo practitioners currently struggle with disjointed physical registers, chaotic WhatsApp chats, and paper prescriptions. They lose 2-3 hours daily managing appointments, tracking patient histories, handling walk-ins, and following up. They need a production-grade, crash-proof clinical operating system that provides an instant branded clinic website, seamless WhatsApp appointment booking, fast (<30s) digital prescriptions, live queue tokens, and reliable subscription billing.

## Demand Evidence
Trial clinics and practicing doctors report immediate daily demand:
1. Doctors need rapid digital prescription generation on phone/tablet (<30s per consultation) with automatic WhatsApp dispatch to patients.
2. Receptionists and clinic staff need instant walk-in token generation and automated booking confirmations to end waiting-room chaos.
3. Doctors want a dedicated branded presence (website link) for their Google Maps and social media profiles that directly accepts appointment bookings.

## Status Quo
Doctors and staff rely on physical registers, fragmented WhatsApp chats, and paper slips. Prescriptions are handwritten, prone to legibility errors, and unsearchable. Patient follow-ups are manually managed or lost. Existing software (e.g., Practo, legacy desktop EMRs) is cumbersome, costly (₹15,000–₹30,000/yr), and lacks integrated personalized doctor branding.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **Target User:** Solo doctors and clinic owners (General Physicians, Pediatricians, Dentists, Dermatologists, Orthopedic specialists) and their front-desk receptionists.
- **Narrowest Wedge:**
  1. Instant branded clinic website with online booking link for Google Maps/social bio.
  2. Automated WhatsApp booking confirmations + <30-second branded prescription generator with instant patient PDF delivery & follow-up reminders.

## Constraints
- Zero breaking changes to existing database schema; forward migrations only.
- 100% clean TypeScript build without suppressing compiler errors (`tsc --noEmit` must pass with 0 errors).
- Fail-closed security on tenant isolation, patient PHI, and prescription signatures.
- Razorpay live & test billing compliance with clear INR pricing (Starter ₹999/mo, Growth ₹2,999/mo).
- Mobile-first responsiveness: receptionists and doctors work on iPads, tablets, and smartphones.

## Premises
1. Doctors adopt ClinicLaunch for two immediate unlocks: (a) instant branded clinic website + booking link for Google Maps/socials, and (b) fast WhatsApp appointment confirmations & <30s digital prescriptions.
2. Production launch requires 100% clean typecheck (fixing the 127 TS errors across templates/functions), crash-proof patient booking/queue, and flawless mobile experience.
3. Doctor onboarding must take <2 minutes from signup to live link, with seamless trial-to-paid Razorpay billing.

## Approaches Considered
### Approach A: Production Hardening & Doctor-Launch Wedge (Chosen)
- **Summary:** Surgically resolve all 127 TypeScript errors across templates, views, and functions; harden the dual wedge (public clinic site + WhatsApp booking + 1-click consultation/prescription + queue token printing); and verify Razorpay billing and fast onboarding end-to-end.
- **Effort:** Medium
- **Risk:** Low
- **Pros:** Full launch readiness for real doctors this week; eliminates runtime crashes; delivers exact dual wedge.
- **Cons:** Defers non-essential enterprise multi-branch features to post-launch.
- **Reuses:** Existing TanStack Start routes, Supabase client/schema, templates registry, Razorpay checkout, WhatsApp notifications.

### Approach B: Minimal Quick-Fix (Rejected)
- **Summary:** Patch only TS compilation errors without end-to-end verification of the doctor onboarding or mobile consultation experience.
- **Rejection Reason:** High risk of doctor abandonment during live clinic hours due to rough UX edges.

### Approach C: Full Architecture Refactor (Rejected)
- **Summary:** Re-architect all 30+ templates into a unified component schema and rebuild multi-tenant pipeline before launching.
- **Rejection Reason:** Delays launch by weeks, over-engineering before gaining feedback from paying doctor cohorts.

## Recommended Approach
Approach A: Production Hardening & Doctor-Launch Wedge. It directly solves the real blockers preventing doctors from using ClinicLaunch in live clinics, guarantees type safety and platform stability, and preserves the working architecture.

## Open Questions
None — all 3 core premises and approach confirmed with the founder.

## Success Criteria
1. `bun x tsc --noEmit` passes with 0 errors (all 127 errors in 42 files resolved).
2. `bun run build` succeeds cleanly.
3. Doctor can sign up/login, configure clinic, and view live public booking link in under 2 minutes.
4. Public booking (`/c/:slug/book`) successfully schedules appointment and triggers WhatsApp confirmation.
5. Doctor consultation view generates and signs digital prescription in <30 seconds.
6. Razorpay billing portal displays accurate pricing, handles subscription checkout, and handles grace/trial transitions without runtime errors.

- WhatsApp API bridge / Webhook service.
